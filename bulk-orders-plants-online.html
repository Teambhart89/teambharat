Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum quantity for a bulk order?

Wholesale pricing starts at 25 plants in a single order. You can mix different plant types and pot sizes — the discount is applied on the total quantity, not per variety.

How long does delivery take in Delhi NCR?

Most in-stock bulk orders are dispatched within 24–72 hours of payment confirmation. Large project orders of 500+ plants, or orders that need specific sizes to be sourced, usually take 5–7 working days and can be delivered in phases.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes. Every bulk order comes with a proper GST tax invoice, so corporate and institutional buyers can claim input credit and complete their procurement paperwork.

What if plants arrive damaged?

Share photos within 7 days of delivery and we replace transit-damaged plants free of cost. Every consignment is packed with root-ball protection and support staking to keep damage rare.

Can you help choose the right plants for our office?

Yes — free consultation is included with every bulk enquiry. Send us photos of the space or share the floor layout, and we will recommend species suited to your light levels, AC exposure and how much watering your team can realistically do.

Do you offer maintenance after delivery?

We offer monthly and quarterly maintenance contracts covering watering, pruning, repotting, pest control and free replacement of plants that die during the contract period. Ask for AMC pricing along with your quotation.

What are the payment terms?

Standard bulk orders are confirmed with an advance and the balance settled before or on delivery. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cheque. Registered corporate buyers can request credit terms against a purchase order.

Do you deliver outside Delhi NCR?

Yes, we ship pan-India by surface transport for orders of 200 plants and above. Freight is charged at actuals and quoted upfront before you confirm the order.
