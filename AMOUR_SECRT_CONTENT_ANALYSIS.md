## 7. Writing Style Guide — With Specific Instructions

### Voice & Tone
Amour Secrt's content voice is: **a knowledgeable, relatable older sister who happens to know everything about lingerie** — honest, warm, never preachy, never salesy, always practical.

### The 5 Rules of Amour Secrt Writing

**Rule 1: Open with a real moment, not a definition.**
❌ WRONG: *"A sports bra is a specialized bra designed to provide support during physical activity."*
✅ RIGHT: *"You've been halfway through a Zumba class when your regular bra starts slipping, digging, and doing everything except supporting you. That's when you realize a sports bra isn't optional — it's essential."*

**Rule 2: Be honest about trade-offs.**
❌ WRONG: *"Push-up bras are the best choice for enhancing your look."*
✅ RIGHT: *"Push-up bras add lift and cleavage — but in 40°C Indian summers, the extra foam can make all-day wear uncomfortable. Here's exactly when to wear them and when not to."*

**Rule 3: Make every tip India-specific.**
❌ WRONG: *"Choose breathable fabrics for summer."*
✅ RIGHT: *"Indian summers mean humidity plus heat — cotton breathes but stretches out after washing, while nylon-spandex keeps its shape but can feel slightly warmer. For most Indian women, a cotton-blend with some lycra is the sweet spot."*

**Rule 4: Never hard-sell. Mention Amour Secrt once or twice maximum, naturally.**
❌ WRONG: *"Buy the Amour Secrt Sports Bra Collection now! Shop our activewear bras today!"*
✅ RIGHT: *"If you're looking for a comfortable activewear bra for Indian summers, the light-support cotton-blend styles at Amour Secrt are worth trying for yoga and daily movement."*

**Rule 5: Give practical, specific answers — not vague advice.**
❌ WRONG: *"Choose a bra that fits well and feels comfortable."*
✅ RIGHT: *"Your band should sit parallel to the ground — not ride up at the back. If it rides up, go one band size down. If the cups overflow or gape, go up or down one cup size. Most women need to adjust both at the same time."*

### Paragraph Length
- Keep paragraphs to 2–4 lines maximum.
- Use bullet points when listing 3 or more items.
- Use H2 for major sections, H3 for sub-points within a section.
- Every H2 section should be self-contained — a reader skimming should understand it without reading everything above.

### Introduction Formula (120–180 words)
1. **Sentence 1–2:** Open with a relatable moment or problem the reader faces.
2. **Sentence 3–4:** Acknowledge the confusion or common mistake.
3. **Sentence 5–6:** State what this article will solve, specifically.
4. **Sentence 7:** Mention the primary keyword naturally.
5. Close with a bridge to the first H2.

### FAQ Section (Required in Every Article)
- Minimum 5 FAQs, maximum 8.
- Write questions exactly how someone would type them into Google.
- Keep answers to 2–4 sentences. Direct, not padded.
- FAQs should target "People Also Ask" boxes in Google search results.

**Good FAQ question formats:**
- "What is the best [product] for Indian women?"
- "How do I know if my [product] fits correctly?"
- "Can I wear [product type] for [activity/outfit]?"
- "How long does a [product] last?"
- "What size [product] should I buy?"

### Conclusion Formula
1. One-sentence summary of the article's main point.
2. Two-sentence practical takeaway the reader can act on today.
3. One soft CTA mentioning Amour Secrt naturally (not a hard sell).

## 11. What Makes Amour Secrt's Content Different From Competitors

Use these as your content differentiators — the things every Amour Secrt article should do that Clovia, Zivame, and Enamor don't consistently do:

### 1. Specificity over generics
Competitors often say: *"Choose a comfortable bra."*  
Amour Secrt says: *"Choose a bra with a band that sits 2 fingers below your bra line, cups that lie flat without wrinkling, and straps you can slide two fingers under — that's comfort, not guesswork."*

### 2. Indian climate context always included
Every relevant article includes a section or mention of India's heat, humidity, monsoon, or AC-to-outdoor temperature swings — because that's a real daily reality for the reader.

### 3. Relatable opening, not textbook opening
Every article starts with a moment, not a definition. The reader should read line one and think: *"Yes, that's exactly me."*

### 4. Honest trade-off acknowledgement
Amour Secrt does not pretend every product works for every woman. Honest guidance — even when it means saying "this product isn't right for you if..." — builds more trust than promotional writing.

### 5. Plain language, no jargon
No medical terminology, no fashion-week vocabulary. If a word needs explaining, either explain it or replace it with a simpler word.

## 12. Things to Never Do

- Do NOT keyword-stuff. The keyword should feel natural, not forced.
- Do NOT use fake statistics or invent numbers.
- Do NOT write generic filler like: *"In today's fast-paced world, women need..."*
- Do NOT hard-sell. Maximum one or two natural brand mentions per article.
- Do NOT copy competitor phrasing — not even structure. Read competitors for research, then close the tab.
- Do NOT write more than 4 lines in a single paragraph.
- Do NOT skip the FAQ section. Every article needs minimum 5 FAQs.
- Do NOT write a conclusion that just repeats the introduction.
- Do NOT end with: *"In conclusion..."* — just write the conclusion.
