## Keyword clusters and intent

### Cluster 1: Core transactional (highest priority)
- online puja booking, book puja online, puja booking online India
- online chadhava, chadawa online, temple chadhava booking
- book pandit online, pandit booking for puja
Intent: transactional. These convert directly and anchor the six service pages.

### Cluster 2: Temple-specific long tail (blog and future landing pages)
- kashi vishwanath puja online booking
- khatu shyam ji chadhava online
- mahakaleshwar bhasma aarti booking
- tirupati online seva booking
- vaishno devi prasad online
Intent: transactional with a named temple. Build one landing page per temple as the business adds temple partners. These are the fastest keywords to rank for because competition is moderate and intent is exact.

### Cluster 3: Problem and intention keywords (content marketing)
- puja for health problems, puja for job promotion
- kaal sarp dosh puja, pitra dosh nivaran puja
- mangal dosh remedies, shani sade sati remedies
- puja for marriage delay, puja for childbirth
Intent: investigational moving to transactional. Answer with blog posts that link to the matching service page.

### Cluster 4: NRI keywords (international pages later)
- online puja for NRI, puja booking from USA
- send prasad to USA / UK / Canada / Australia
- hindu priest online ceremony, online pandit for NRI wedding
Intent: transactional from abroad. The service pages already speak to NRIs; dedicated country pages can follow.

### Cluster 5: Astrology
- talk to astrologer online, online kundli reading
- kundli milan for marriage, janam kundli analysis
- numerology by date of birth, vastu tips for home
Intent: mixed. The astrology page targets consultation intent; blog content captures informational queries.

## Generative AI (AEO/GEO) optimization applied

AI answer engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) favor content that:
1. Answers a question directly in the first sentence of a section. Every H2/H3 section on the service pages opens with a direct answer.
2. Uses FAQPage and Service schema. Both ship in the theme (`inc/schema.php`).
3. States who, what, where explicitly. Pages name the service, the temples, the countries served and the proof mechanism (video, photos, live stream).
4. Uses natural conversational phrasing that matches spoken queries, for example "Will the puja be performed in my name even if I am not present?"

## On-page rules the content follows

- One H1 per page (the page title), keyword at the start.
- H2 for major sections, H3 for sub-sections, H4 for supporting notes. No level skipping.
- Primary keyword in: H1, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, URL slug.
- No keyword stuffing: each keyword variant appears where it reads naturally, roughly 1 to 2 percent density.
- Minimal use of dashes; plain, warm, reader-first sentences.
- Internal links: every service page links to contact and other services via the header, footer and CTA bands.
