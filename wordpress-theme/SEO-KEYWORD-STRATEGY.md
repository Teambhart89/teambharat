# SEO Keyword Research & Content Strategy
### Avdesh Kumar | SEO & AI Search Optimization Specialist, Delhi India

This document maps the target keywords used across the website, the SEO friendly URL
for each service, and the search intent behind every page. Every keyword below is woven
naturally into the page copy, headings (H1 to H4), meta titles, and meta descriptions
so the site reads well for humans and ranks well in search engines and AI answer engines.

---

## 1. Keyword Research Method

Keywords were grouped by **search intent** and **topic cluster** so the site follows a
clean pillar and cluster structure. This is the structure that both Google and modern
LLM platforms (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) reward, because
it signals genuine topical authority instead of scattered pages.

- **Pillar pages** target broad, high value head terms.
- **Service (cluster) pages** target mid tail commercial terms.
- **Blog posts** (folder ready) target long tail informational terms and feed internal links back to the money pages.

Intent buckets used:

| Intent | Example queries | Page type |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| Commercial | "seo expert in delhi", "hire seo specialist" | Home, Service pages |
| Transactional | "seo services", "google ads management" | Service pages |
| Informational | "what is generative engine optimization" | Blog / FAQ blocks |
| Navigational | "avdesh kumar seo" | Home, About |

## 7. On-Page Optimization Applied to Every Service Page

Each service page in this theme ships with:

1. **One H1** containing the primary keyword and, where natural, the location.
2. **H2** section headings for each major topic (What, How, Why, Platforms, FAQ).
3. **H3** sub-headings for individual features, process steps, and FAQ questions.
4. **H4** headings for finer detail inside feature and benefit blocks.
5. A unique **meta title** (under ~60 characters where possible) and **meta description** (~150 to 160 characters).
6. **FAQ schema (JSON-LD)** for rich results and AI answer eligibility.
7. **Service + Person schema** so search engines understand who offers what.
8. **Internal links** to related services and the contact page.
9. Natural keyword density. No stuffing, no unnatural repetition, white-hat only.

## 8. Content Principles (human-first, AI-optimized)

- Short paragraphs and plain language so readers connect quickly.
- Direct, quotable answers near the top of each section (good for featured snippets and AI Overviews).
- Genuine value: every page explains *what you get* and *what it changes for the business*.
- No excessive dashes. Clean, natural sentences.
- Consistent entity naming ("Avdesh Kumar", "SEO and AI Search Optimization Specialist") so search engines and LLMs build a stable knowledge graph entry.
