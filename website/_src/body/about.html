How we ended up doing more than selling plants

We started where most nurseries start, growing and supplying plants. The work that followed came from watching what happened to those plants afterwards.

A client would order forty plants for a new office. They would arrive healthy. Six months later we would be back on the same floor, quoting to replace half of them, and the reason was never the plants. It was that nobody had been given the job of watering them, or the areca palm had been placed in a windowless corner because that was where the gap in the furniture was.

So we learned the rest of it. Soil, drainage, irrigation, pest management, and how to design a planting that a real office or a real family can actually keep alive. Today the supply side and the service side are the same team, which is the only arrangement that stops plants falling into the gap between them.

What we believe about plants

The site decides, not the catalogue

Nearly every plant that fails in Delhi NCR failed because it was put somewhere it was never going to survive. Light is the constraint people underestimate most, followed by who is actually going to water it. We ask about both before we quote.

Fewer plants, better placed

Most spaces we visit want more plants than they can support. A smaller number in the right positions, kept healthy, looks better than a full plant list slowly declining. This costs us revenue on the first order and earns it back on the fifth.

Say the inconvenient thing early

If your terrace needs waterproofing before it needs a design, if your reception has no light for a living wall, if the species you have set your heart on will not last a Delhi summer, you should hear it at the quotation stage. It is a shorter conversation then.

Ground preparation is not the boring part

It is the part that determines whether the garden still looks right in year three. On most of our landscaping projects, more of the budget goes below the surface than into the planting, and clients who understand why are the ones who end up happiest.
