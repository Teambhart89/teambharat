What event plant rental covers

You tell us the venue, the date and the look. We bring grown plants in finished planters, place them to your decorator's layout, and return afterwards to take everything away. Delivery, placement and removal are quoted as one line, so there is no separate labour charge appearing on the day.

Weddings and receptions

Entrance avenues, mandap and stage surrounds, walkway edging, seating areas and photo corners. Large plants in matched pairs still do more for an entrance than any quantity of scattered small ones, which is worth knowing when a budget is being split.

Exhibitions and trade shows

Stand dressing that makes a booth read as established rather than assembled that morning. We work to the venue build up and break down slots, move through service entrances, and keep the stand clear of soil and water. On multi day shows we can come back mid run to water and tidy, which matters by day three in a hot hall.

Product launches and corporate events

Stage and backdrop planting, reception areas and press walls. Usually a short window for setup and a hard deadline, which is fine as long as we know about it when quoting rather than on the morning.

Film, television and photo shoots

Hire by the day, early delivery for set builds, and a team used to working around a crew. Tell us what the camera needs to see, because the plant that reads best on screen is not always the healthiest one in the nursery.

Temporary and project offices

Short duration office greening where a full rental contract does not fit. For anything running beyond a month, our office plant rental works out better on price and includes ongoing care.

The plants that do the work at events

For height and presence

Areca palm, raphis palm and tall dracaena. These are what make an entrance feel like an entrance and a stage feel finished.

For mass and screening

Ficus and dense medium foliage, used to hide what you would rather guests did not look at. Generator cables, service doors and the back of a temporary structure all disappear behind a run of matched plants.

For colour

Seasonal flowering plants where the date falls in the right months, and coloured foliage such as croton where it does not. We are straightforward about this. Plants do not read the run sheet, and no supplier can promise a specific flower in full bloom on an exact date. Where an exact colour is critical, we build it into the planter and the foliage instead.

Planters matter more than people expect

The pot is visible at eye level all evening while the plant is often seen against a crowd. Matched planters in a finish that suits the palette lift a plant list more cheaply than upgrading every plant would.

Booking, timing and what we need from you

  1. Tell us the date first

    Even before the plant list is settled. Holding a date costs you nothing and peak wedding season stock gets committed early.

  2. Share the venue and the layout

    Venue name, access details and your decorator's layout if there is one. Photographs of the space are enough to start.

  3. Approve the plant list and quote

    A list by position with the hire charge, delivery, placement and removal included in a single figure.

  4. Setup on the day

    We arrive in the slot the venue allows and place everything to the agreed layout, then step out of your decorator's way.

  5. Removal afterwards

    Collection at the agreed time, including late night after a wedding, which is normal for us and carries no premium.

How much notice we need

Two to three weeks is comfortable through peak wedding season from November to February. Outside that window a week is usually plenty, and smaller orders can often be managed at a day or two of notice. If your requirement is urgent, call rather than email.

Costs, damage and the practical details

Hire is charged per plant per day and varies mainly with plant size and planter finish. There is a practical minimum order, because sending a vehicle and a crew across the NCR for three plants does not work, though if we already have a delivery going to the same area on your date we can usually add a small requirement to it.

Some scuffing at a busy event is expected and never charged. Plants broken, knocked over and left, or removed from the venue are charged at replacement value, and this is set out on the rental note before the event rather than raised afterwards.

If you decide after the event that the plants should stay, say so. We adjust the hire charge against a purchase price rather than charging you twice for the same plant. For larger permanent requirements, our wholesale supply is the better route.
