How we work

We ask about the site before the plants

Light, water, air conditioning and who will actually be looking after them. Almost every plant that fails in Delhi NCR failed because it was put somewhere it was never going to survive, not because it was a poor plant.

We will talk you out of things

If a species will not last in your lobby, or your terrace needs waterproofing before it needs a design, you will hear it at the quotation stage rather than the following summer.

We stay after the delivery

Supply and care sit with the same team, so there is no gap where a healthy plant is handed to nobody in particular. One number covers the order, the site visit and anything that goes wrong.
