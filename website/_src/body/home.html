Why buyers stay

We would rather sell you fewer plants that live

Almost every plant that fails in Delhi NCR failed because it was put somewhere it was never going to survive. Light is the constraint people underestimate most, followed by who is actually going to water it. We ask about both before we quote.

  • Nursery sourcing without a middleman. You buy from the people growing the plants, which is where the price difference comes from.
  • Hardened stock. Plants are acclimatised before dispatch, so the move to your site is not the shock that kills them.
  • Species matched to the site. We will talk you out of a plant rather than sell it to you twice.
  • Clean paperwork. GST invoice on every order, with purchase order references where procurement needs them.
  • One point of contact. The same number for the quote, the dispatch and anything that goes wrong.

How it works

From first call to plants on site, usually inside a week

  1. Share the requirement

    Quantity, site type and timeline. Photographs of the space help more than a description does.

  2. Free consultation

    We suggest species suited to the light, water and air conditioning where they will actually stand.

  3. Written quotation

    Itemised, valid fifteen days, with no charges appearing later that were not on it.

  4. Selection and packing

    Hand picked, hardened for transit, packed with root ball protection. Photographs before dispatch on request.

  5. Delivery and care

    Placed on site with a written care sheet, and a maintenance contract if you want one.
