What to include in your enquiry

You will get a faster and far more accurate answer if the first message covers these. None of it needs to be precise.

  • The space. An office floor, a terrace, a society garden, a wedding venue. Photographs beat descriptions every time.
  • Rough size or quantity. Floor area, square footage, or an approximate plant count.
  • Location. The pin code is enough. It tells us delivery, travel and whether we can service it properly.
  • Your timeline. Particularly if there is a fixed date such as an event, an office opening or a festival.
  • Who will look after the plants. This changes our recommendation more than almost anything else.
