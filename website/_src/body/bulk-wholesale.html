Who buys plants from us in bulk

Bulk supply is not one kind of customer. A landscaper ordering three hundred shrubs for a site and an HR team ordering forty desk plants want completely different things from us, and the only thing they share is that neither should be paying single unit prices.

Landscapers and garden contractors

You need consistent stock, predictable sizes and a rate you can quote your own client from. Trade buyers get a standing rate list rather than a fresh quotation every week, and we hold regular varieties so a repeat order does not turn into a sourcing exercise.

Corporate offices and workspaces

Reception statements, cabin plants, meeting room greenery and something at the end of every corridor. We suggest species that survive continuous air conditioning and a floor plate with limited daylight, which rules out a surprising amount of what looks good in a catalogue.

Builders, societies and facility teams

Sample flats, club houses, entrance plazas and common greens, usually against a budget that has already been approved and a date that has already been announced. Large orders can be delivered in phases so your site is not storing plants it cannot plant yet.

Hotels, cafes and restaurants

Hardy indoor species that hold up to heavy footfall, constant air conditioning and the occasional spilled drink, chosen to sit with your interior palette rather than argue with it.

Schools, hospitals and institutions

Campus planting, herbal and medicinal gardens for teaching, and air purifying plants for classrooms, wards and waiting areas. Institutional buyers usually need a formal quotation and GST invoice for their procurement process, which we provide as standard.

Retail nurseries and florists

Wholesale stock for resale, in the sizes and quantities that move through a retail counter. If you buy from us regularly, a running account saves a payment cycle on every order.

What you can order at wholesale rates

Everything in our range is available in bulk, and you can mix categories within a single order to reach a quantity slab. There is no requirement to order twenty five of one variety.

Indoor plants

The bulk of office and hospitality orders. These are the varieties that tolerate low light and steady air conditioning without slowly thinning out.

Most requested indoor varieties

Money plant, snake plant, ZZ plant, aglaonema, syngonium, philodendron, peace lily, dracaena, areca palm and raphis palm.

Outdoor and landscaping stock

Ficus, ashoka, bottle palm, bougainvillea, hedge and border plants, ground cover and lawn grass. Sizes run from small liners for mass planting up to established specimens for entrances and feature positions.

A note on specimen sizes

Large specimens are worth ordering early. They are the item most likely to be out of stock at short notice, and growing one on to size is not something we can do inside your project timeline.

Flowering and seasonal plants

Winter and summer seasonals, roses, hibiscus and marigold, used heavily by societies and event teams. Availability follows the growing calendar rather than the order date, so plan seasonal colour a few weeks ahead.

Succulents, cacti and small plants

The volume category for corporate gifting and for anyone furnishing a lot of desks at once. Low maintenance, compact, and forgiving of the watering habits of an average office.

Pots, planters and growing supplies

Ceramic, fibre, FRP, metal and plastic planters, plus potting mix, cocopeat, vermicompost, fertiliser, pebbles and basic tools. Ordering plants and planters together usually saves a delivery and always saves the argument about which pot fits which plant.

What moves the final rate

Species we grow in volume ourselves carry the deepest discount. Imported and slow growing varieties carry the least. Pot choice can change the line item more than the plant does, and a delivery to a site with no vehicle access costs more to complete than one with a loading bay.

How to place a bulk order

  1. Share the requirement

    Call or send a message with the quantity, the type of site and your timeline. Photographs of the space help more than a description does.

  2. Free plant consultation

    We suggest species suited to the light, water and air conditioning at your site. Where a plant you have asked for will not survive there, we say so before you buy it.

  3. Written quotation

    An itemised quote showing plant, pot, quantity, rate and GST. Valid for fifteen days, with no charges appearing later that were not on it.

  4. Selection and packing

    Plants are hand picked from the nursery, hardened for transit and packed with root ball protection. We will send photographs of the actual stock before dispatch if you ask.

  5. Delivery and placement

    Delivered to site and placed where they need to go, with a written care sheet for whoever will be looking after them.

Delivery across Delhi NCR and beyond

Inside the National Capital Region

We deliver to Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and the surrounding belt including Sonipat and Manesar. Stocked varieties usually reach site within 24 to 72 hours of confirmation.

Outside the NCR

For orders of roughly two hundred plants and above we ship pan India by surface transport, with freight charged at actuals and quoted before you confirm. Below that quantity the freight generally costs more than the wholesale discount saves, and we will tell you so rather than take the order.
