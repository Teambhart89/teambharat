# Keyword Research and URL Map
### Online Plants Cart, Delhi NCR

**About the data in this document.** No paid keyword tool (SemRush, Ahrefs, DataForSEO, Keyword Planner) was connected to this session, so nothing here carries a verified monthly search volume. Every keyword below was instead pulled from live search results: the phrases real competitors in Delhi NCR actually rank for and write into their page titles, headings and service names. Priority is judged on commercial intent and competitive gap, not volume.

**Before you invest in content beyond this site, verify volumes** in Google Keyword Planner set to India, and check Search Console after 60 days to see which of these actually pull impressions. Reprioritise from that real data.

Two patterns are worth acting on:

1. **Almost every competitor targets one city name at a time.** Pages are built for "Gurgaon" or "Noida" separately rather than "Delhi NCR" as a blob. The site follows that pattern by naming cities inside headings and body copy where it reads naturally.
2. **Rental and maintenance keywords are far less crowded than "buy plants online".** Retail plant keywords are dominated by national players like Nurserylive and Ugaoo, which a regional nursery will struggle to outrank. Service keywords tied to Delhi NCR are winnable. The site is weighted accordingly.

## URL structure

All service URLs sit one level under `/services/`, use lowercase words separated by single hyphens, contain the primary keyword, and carry no dates, IDs or stop words.

```
onlineplantscart.in/
onlineplantscart.in/services/
onlineplantscart.in/services/bulk-wholesale-plants-delhi-ncr/
onlineplantscart.in/services/office-plants-on-rent/
onlineplantscart.in/services/landscaping-and-garden-design/
onlineplantscart.in/services/plant-maintenance-services/
onlineplantscart.in/services/vertical-garden-and-green-wall/
onlineplantscart.in/services/terrace-and-balcony-garden-design/
onlineplantscart.in/services/plants-on-rent-for-events/
onlineplantscart.in/services/corporate-plant-gifting/
onlineplantscart.in/about/
onlineplantscart.in/contact/
```

Why these choices:

- **Trailing slash, no `.html`.** Cleaner to share, and it lets you add `/services/office-plants-on-rent/gurgaon/` later without restructuring.
- **Location only where it earns its place.** `bulk-wholesale-plants-delhi-ncr` carries the region because that is how people search for wholesale supply. `landscaping-and-garden-design` does not, because the page needs to stay usable when you later build city variants under it.
- **No keyword repetition across URLs.** Two URLs competing for the same phrase split their own ranking signal. Each URL below owns exactly one primary keyword.

## Internal linking plan

Link equity flows in a hub and spoke pattern with cross links between related services.

```
Home
 ├─→ Services hub ──→ all 8 service pages
 └─→ 3 featured services directly

Bulk plants  ←→  Landscaping        (a landscaper needs both)
Bulk plants  ←→  Corporate gifting  (both are volume orders)
Office rent  ←→  Plant maintenance  (maintenance is inside the rental)
Landscaping  ←→  Terrace gardens    (same team, different scale)
Landscaping  ←→  Vertical gardens   (usually quoted together)
Event rental ←→  Office rent        (same rental logistics)

Every service page ──→ Contact
Every service page ──→ Services hub (via breadcrumb)
```

Every internal link on the site uses descriptive anchor text such as "office plants on rent" rather than "click here" or a bare URL.

## What is deliberately not targeted

Skipping these keeps the site focused and avoids pages that will never rank:

- **Generic retail terms** like "buy indoor plants online" or "money plant price". Nurserylive, Ugaoo and Amazon own these nationally. Your product pages on the store can chase them; these service pages should not.
- **Plant care information keywords** like "how to care for a snake plant". High volume, near zero commercial intent, and answered by thousands of blogs already. Worth a blog later, not the main site now.
- **Single city pages built now.** Building `/gurgaon/`, `/noida/` and `/delhi/` variants of every service before the parent pages have any authority creates thin duplicate content. Build them in phase two, only for the two or three services that show real traction in Search Console.

## Suggested next phase

Once these pages are indexed and you have 60 days of Search Console data:

1. Add city variants only under services that already show impressions.
2. Add a project gallery with real photos and real client names. Nothing beats proof in this category.
3. Start a small blog aimed at the long tail questions listed above, one post per month, each linking up to its parent service page.
4. Build a Google Business Profile and keep the name, address and phone identical to what appears in the site footer and schema. Inconsistent details here quietly damage local ranking.
